Interview App

A practice coach for interviews, a transparent note-taker for meetings, and a job search that ranks openings against your CV and prepares each application for your approval. A team of AI agents runs on your own PC with Ollama on your GPU. Claude is optional.

  • CV-matched job search

What it does

Three tools, one agent platform

Find the jobs that fit you, prepare for a specific one, practise out loud with feedback, and keep honest notes of the meetings you're allowed to record. All of it on your machine.

Interview Coach

  • Paste your resume and a job description. Agents build a prep pack: your profile, the role's competencies, fit and honest gaps, STAR stories drafted from your real experience, and about 20 likely questions.
  • Spoken mock interviews. The interviewer reads each question aloud. You answer by voice or by typing, with live structure and delivery hints. Hints appear only in practice.
  • A coach's debrief for every answer: rubric scores (structure, relevance, specificity, impact, communication, plus technical depth for technical questions), quotes from your answer as evidence, specific fixes, a stronger structure, and a follow-up question when something's missing.
  • Session reports and spaced repetition. Drills target your weakest areas, and the next session starts with the competencies that are weak or due.

Meeting Notes

  • Consent first. You confirm everyone knows they're being transcribed. A copyable disclosure line is provided, and a visible indicator shows when audio is captured.
  • Live transcript: Me from your mic, Them from the shared meeting tab or screen, next to your own notepad. You can search what was said and ask it to catch you up.
  • Enhanced notes when you stop. Your notes stay as you wrote them. AI additions are marked and linked to the transcript, with decisions, action items and open questions.
  • After the meeting: ask questions with citations, draft a follow-up email or to-do list, export Markdown, or delete the last few minutes.

Jobs: search and apply, with you in charge

  • Searches the source. Open roles come from 55 company job boards through their official public APIs (Anthropic, OpenAI, Databricks, Stripe, Razorpay, CRED, Meesho, Freshworks, Bosch and more). Paste any Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby or SmartRecruiters careers link to add a company. No LinkedIn or Indeed automation.
  • Ranks by your CV. Every job gets a score you can read: title, skills from your CV, location, remote and visa fit, seniority and recency. An AI reviewer then checks the best ones requirement by requirement and quotes your CV as evidence. Quotes it can't find in your CV don't count.
  • Drafts from facts. Each application gets a tailored cover letter and answers drafted only from your CV. A fact-checker flags any number, employer, degree or skill your CV doesn't support. Legal, consent and diversity questions are never answered by AI.
  • You approve, you submit. You review and approve each one (10 a day by default). On request, a browser assistant fills the employer's own form in Edge or Chrome, attaches your CV and stops. You press Submit. It never solves CAPTCHAs or ticks consent boxes.

They connect. One click turns a job you applied for into an interview prep pack. After a real interview, record or type your own debrief with the Interview debrief template. One click turns the questions you were asked into practice questions for that job.

How it works

The workflows, end to end

Each workflow is a fixed pipeline. Code orchestrates the specialist agents, and every step finishes even when no AI model is available.

CoachPrepare and practise

  1. Add a target

    Paste or upload your resume and the job description.

  2. Prep pack

    Five agents analyse you and the role, then draft stories and questions.

  3. Mock interview

    Questions are read aloud. You answer out loud with live practice hints.

  4. Debrief

    Rate yourself, then see the scores, evidence and a stronger structure.

  5. Session report

    Patterns across your answers, 2–4 drills, and what to practise next.

  6. Spaced repetition

    Each competency's score and review date are updated.

The next session puts weak and due competencies first, weighted by what the job cares about.

NotesCapture and remember

  1. Consent

    Confirm everyone knows. Copy the disclosure line into the chat.

  2. Live capture

    Transcript labelled Me and Them, alongside your own notepad.

  3. Search and catch up

    Find where something was said, or get a recap of the last few minutes.

  4. Enhanced notes

    On Stop, your notes are expanded, with each AI addition linked to its source.

  5. Ask and draft

    Cited answers across saved meetings, plus email and to-do drafts.

JobsFind and apply

  1. Your CV

    Upload it once. Add where you can work and what you're looking for.

  2. Search

    About 10,000 open jobs from company boards, read through official APIs.

  3. Rank

    A readable score for each, then an AI check of the best against your CV.

  4. Draft and fact-check

    Cover letter and answers from your CV. Unsupported claims are flagged.

  5. You approve

    Edit, answer the questions that are yours, approve. 10 a day by default.

  6. You submit

    The browser assistant fills the form and stops. You press Submit.

Jobs you apply to or dismiss tune the next search. Answers you type are remembered, and legal ones only for the same country.

LoopDebrief into practice

  1. Real interview

    You interview as yourself. The app isn't used during it.

  2. Your debrief

    Afterwards, dictate or type what you were asked and how it went.

  3. Questions extracted

    The notes agent lists the questions you were asked.

  4. Into your practice bank

    One click adds them to that job's questions for the next session.

Reliability

Built to finish, and to improve

It runs end to end without you stepping in. It recovers from errors on its own, and it learns from your sessions and feedback.

When something goes wrong

  • Validated output. Every answer is checked against a schema. Out-of-range scores are clamped, and invented quotes are rejected.
  • Repair, then salvage. The model is shown its exact mistake and asked again. On the last attempt, mostly good output is kept and fixed in code.
  • Fallback chain. Ollama → Claude (or the reverse), with retries and backoff. Circuit breakers skip a backend that's down.
  • Always an answer. Deadlines keep the screen responsive. If no model answers, built-in rules finish the step, and the result is labelled.
  • Resumable jobs. Pipelines save their progress, so a crash or restart picks up where it left off.
  • Speech fallback. GPU → CPU → the browser's own recognition (with a notice).

How it gets better

  • It learns you. Every answer updates that competency's score and review date, so practice goes where it helps most.
  • It learns from feedback. A 👍 or 👎 with a reason becomes guidance in that agent's instructions. "Too harsh" or "too lenient" adds a small, capped calibration that is shown to you.
  • It learns which model works. Each agent tries the backend that has been giving it usable results.
  • It's measured. A set of hand-scored answers checks the evaluator before you change models or prompts. Every run is traced.
  • Real interviews feed practice. Debrief questions go straight into your question bank.

Install & run locally

Install on Windows

The app runs on your PC and opens in your browser at http://127.0.0.1:8765. That address only works on the computer running the app.

Option A: one command

Open PowerShell and run:

powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -c "irm https://manish-genai.github.io/interview-app-site/install.ps1 | iex"
  1. Checks for Git and Python and offers to install them with winget if they're missing.
  2. Downloads the app into D:\Interview APP, or %USERPROFILE%\Interview APP if there's no D: drive. The source repository is private, so Git asks you to sign in to GitHub once (Git Credential Manager opens a browser window).
  3. Installs the Python packages, local Whisper speech-to-text and the Ollama models. It offers to install Ollama if it's missing.
  4. Adds an Interview App shortcut to your desktop and starts the app.

Option B: step by step

With Git and Python already installed, run these one at a time:

git clone https://github.com/Manish-GenAI/Interview-App.git "D:\Interview APP"
cd "D:\Interview APP"
scripts\setup.bat
scripts\start.bat

Keep the start window open. When it says Ready, your browser opens http://127.0.0.1:8765. Use the desktop shortcut or scripts\start.bat next time.

This site is public, the code is not. GitHub Pages sites are public even when the repository is private, so anyone with the link can see this page, the docs and the demo.

The demo keeps everything you type in your own browser and sends nothing to a server. The exception is the microphone: it uses your browser's speech recognition, which in Chrome and Edge is a cloud service.

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11 (Linux, macOS and WSL also work with scripts/setup.sh)
  • Python 3.11 or 3.12, and Git
  • Chrome or Edge (needed to capture meeting audio)
  • Ollama for local AI (recommended)
  • An NVIDIA GPU is recommended. An RTX 4080 (16 GB) runs everything comfortably

Recommended for an RTX 4080

Main modelqwen3:14b (default, pulled by setup). It reliably follows JSON and fits in 16 GB alongside Whisper

Newer models such as qwen3.5:9b or gemma4:12b can be chosen in Settings if ollama list shows them.

Claude (optional)

Add an Anthropic API key in Settings and choose the order: Ollama first with Claude as fallback, or the reverse. The default model is claude-opus-5-5. A monthly budget guard (default $50, adjustable) switches back to Ollama when it's reached. Only the text of each request is sent.

Privacy & responsible use

Practise for the interview. Be honest in the meeting and in every application.

It's built for preparation, transparent note-taking and a truthful job search. There's no stealth mode, it doesn't feed answers during live interviews or meetings, and it never submits an application for you.

Transparent by design

  • Consent is confirmed before every meeting, a Transcribing indicator stays visible, and a disclosure line is ready to paste.
  • Live hints exist only in practice sessions. During meetings you can only search or recap what was already said. AI Q&A unlocks once the meeting is saved.
  • It doesn't hide from screen sharing or recording, and it never joins calls as a bot.
  • Applications: every one is approved by you and submitted by you. Drafts come only from your CV and are fact-checked. Legal, consent and voluntary diversity questions are always yours, and text in a posting that's aimed at AI tools is shown to you.

Your data stays yours

  • Everything is stored locally. Audio is never stored, only text.
  • Delete the last few minutes of a transcript at any time, delete whole meetings, or set retention days.
  • Nothing leaves your PC unless you turn on Claude, and then only the text of each request is sent.
  • Before any model reads your CV, your name, contact details and personal attributes are removed. The app never stores diversity answers.

FAQ

Questions and troubleshooting

Why doesn't http://127.0.0.1:8765 open on my phone or another computer?

127.0.0.1 always means "this device". The link works only on the PC where the app is running (scripts\start.bat or the desktop shortcut), while its window stays open. To try the app anywhere else, use the live demo.

The app says "Built-in rules". How do I get AI results?

No model is reachable yet. Install and start Ollama, then run ollama pull qwen3:14b, or add a Claude API key in Settings. Then open System → Test connections.

Do I need a GPU?

No, but it helps a lot. Without one, Ollama runs slowly on the CPU, Whisper uses a small English model on the CPU, and you can use Claude or the built-in rules instead. With an RTX 4080, everything runs fast and privately on your PC.

Where is my data stored?

In the app's data folder, e.g. D:\Interview APP\data. It holds the database, your settings, your API key (if you add one) and downloaded speech models. Audio is never saved.

What does it cost?

Running locally with Ollama costs nothing beyond your electricity. Claude is optional and billed by Anthropic per request. The app estimates spend and stops using Claude at your monthly budget.

Can I use it during a real interview?

No. It's for preparing beforehand and debriefing afterwards. It has no live-answer feature and no stealth mode. Using hidden AI help in an interview misrepresents your abilities and usually breaks the hiring process's rules.

Does it apply to jobs automatically?

No. It finds and ranks jobs, drafts each application from your CV and fact-checks it, but you approve every application, and you press Submit on the employer's own form. The browser assistant only types the answers you approved and attaches your CV. It never submits, never solves CAPTCHAs, and never ticks consent boxes. There's a daily limit (10 by default), because a few well-matched, truthful applications do better than a flood of generic ones.

Which job sites does it search?

Company job boards on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and SmartRecruiters, through the public APIs their own career pages use. 55 companies are included (AI labs, Indian startups such as Razorpay, CRED and Meesho, and large tech and SaaS companies), and you can add any other by pasting its careers link. Remotive adds remote jobs, credited with a link back. It doesn't scrape or automate LinkedIn, Indeed or Naukri, because their terms forbid it. You can paste a job from there by hand.

Will it answer visa, salary or diversity questions for me?

No. Work authorisation and sponsorship are answered only with what you typed, remembered for jobs in the same country. Your salary expectation comes only from your profile, and salary history is left to you. Consents and voluntary self-identification (gender, race, veteran, disability) are always answered by you in the form, and the app never stores them.

Meeting audio isn't captured

In the browser's share dialog, tick Share tab audio for browser-based calls. For the desktop Zoom or Teams apps, share Entire screen and tick Share system audio. Use headphones so the other side isn't picked up twice by your mic.
